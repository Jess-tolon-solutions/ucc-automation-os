# Session State — Monday Oct 5, 2026

## Headline finding: account-wide broken link tracking

Every link in every email sent from the B2C GoHighLevel sub-account has been
non-functional. This is not specific to our workflows.

### Evidence
- GHL rewrites all email links through `email.visitors.uppercervicalcare.com`
- That hostname has **no DNS records at all** (verified via public resolvers)
- Parent `visitors.uppercervicalcare.com` resolves **MX only**, pointing at
  Mailgun (GHL's sending infrastructure). No A or CNAME record.
- GHL's Domains screen shows the domain as "All Set" because it only validates
  mail delivery, never the click-tracking host
- Decoded a live tracking URL and confirmed the destination inside the wrapper
  was correct, including fully resolved merge fields. The build was never wrong.

### Scope of impact
- 21 patients received the rerouting choice email with a dead button
- Zero click data exists because clicks were never recordable
- Affects newsletters and any other B2C email, not just automation

### Workarounds attempted and ruled out
- Per-email link tracking toggle: does not prevent rewriting
- Account-level disable: GHL support confirms no such setting exists
- Plain-text URLs instead of buttons: **also rewritten**, verified empirically

### The fix
One CNAME record for `email.visitors` pointing at GHL's tracking target.
Exact value is shown in GHL under Settings > Domains > visitors.uppercervicalcare.com.

DNS is managed at `ns1.directorysecure.com` / `ns2.directorysecure.com`,
not a mainstream registrar. May require whoever manages UCC hosting rather
than Billy directly.

## Built today

### Cascade Result workflow (B2C)
Inbound webhook trigger. Structure:
1. Create/Update Contact — Email from `lead_email`
2. Add tag `patient-wants-reroute`
3. Find Opportunity — Patient Leads, most recently created
4. Condition, four branches:
   - **Auto accepted**: status=matched AND has_level2=true -> stage Accepted, tag `lead-accepted`
   - **Awaiting response**: status=matched -> stage Rerouting
   - **Nobody in range**: status=no_doctors_in_range -> stage Closed - No Match, close-the-loop email, tag `patient-closed-no-response`
   - **None**: ends, catches unanticipated status values

### Patient choice email
Converted from branded template to plain email written directly in the
Patient Expiration Watch step. Removes the template-snapshot problem where
step content did not update when the template changed.

URL in body (swap in once DNS is fixed):
`https://zip-cascade.ucc-automation.workers.dev/r/{{inboundWebhookRequest.lead_id}}`

## Decisions made

- **Plain text emails are the standard for patient touchpoints**, not branded
  templates. Better deliverability, matches the conversational UCC voice, and
  removes the template-snapshot failure mode. Branded templates were only ever
  needed for buttons, which we no longer use.
- **Patient Chose Reroute workflow is now dormant.** The click goes straight to
  the Worker, so GHL never sees it. Tagging and stage movement moved to
  Cascade Result.
- **BD confirmation emails stay ON** until GHL's emails are verified working.
  Switching them off now would leave patients with only dead-link emails.
- **Round 2 doctor emails deferred** to a separate workflow because
  `matched_doctor_emails` is an array and GHL cannot loop it. Worker will POST
  once per doctor.
